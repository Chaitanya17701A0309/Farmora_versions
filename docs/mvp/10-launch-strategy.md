# Farmora: Social Media Launch Strategy (Zero Budget)

Covers deliverable 18. The full long-term plan is in [docs/08-marketing-plan.md](../08-marketing-plan.md). This document is the **₹0 launch playbook** for the MVP.

**Campaign line:** **"Families Decide. Farmers Grow. Everyone Benefits."**
**Launch hashtag:** `#VoteForYourFood` · secondary `#FamiliesDecide` `#KnowYourFarmer`

## 1. Core Idea: The vote is the marketing

Every vote is shareable. A family voting for Foxtail Millet gets a ready-made card:

> 🌾 *"I voted for Foxtail Millet on Farmora. Families decide what farmers grow next season. Add your vote →"* `farmora.in/demand?ref=LAK8X2`

Sharing to WhatsApp, Instagram Stories or Facebook brings new families. The leaderboard creates friendly competition ("Can Ragi beat Groundnut in Hyderabad?").

## 5. Zero-Cost Growth Tactics
1. **Shareable vote cards:** auto-generated OG image per item and region (`/api/og?item=…`).
2. **Referral codes:** `?ref=CODE` on every share link; points on the referee's first order.
3. **Community ambassadors:** 1 volunteer per apartment complex or village; recognition badge, early access, free product samples from farmers.
4. **Barter influencer deals:** 10–50k-follower regional food, parenting and fitness creators in exchange for farm products.
5. **Farmer-made content:** farmers film 30-sec updates on their own phones, and Farmora edits and posts them.
6. **PR hooks:** "India's first platform where families vote on what farmers grow"; monthly "Demand Report" data stories for journalists.
7. **Partnerships:** FPOs, KVKs, agriculture colleges, NGOs, residents' welfare associations, school PTAs (family health angle, adults only).
8. **SEO:** public leaderboard pages per district (`/demand/hyderabad`) rank for "millets demand Hyderabad", "organic groundnut near me".

## 8. Guardrails
- WhatsApp: only opt-in channels and groups where you're a member. No bulk unsolicited messages.
- Children: no targeted content or data collection; family content is addressed to parents.
- Farmer and family photos only with written consent.
- Sponsored or barter content is always disclosed (#ad / #collab) per ASCI guidelines.
- Never promise prices or guaranteed purchases. Votes and pre-order interest are non-binding.
