# 8. Marketing & Social Media Plan

**Goal:** Reach every farmer, every family and every child, starting on day 1 of development, not on launch day.

| Audience | What they need to hear | Primary channels |
|---|---|---|
| **Farmers** | "Earn more, sell direct, grow smarter, in your language" | WhatsApp, YouTube, village meetings, FPOs, KVKs, local radio, field agents |
| **Families (customers)** | "Pure, traceable food straight from the farmer who grew it" | Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp communities, influencers, apartment societies |
| **Children** | "Where does my food come from?" Learning and pride in farming | Schools, farm visits, educational content **delivered via parents and teachers** |

> **Children, responsibly:** under India's DPDP Act 2023, data from users under 18 needs verifiable parental consent, and tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads aimed at children are prohibited. Farmora reaches children **only through education** (schools, parents, family content). It never runs targeted ads at kids or collects their data.

## 8.3 Farmer Outreach: Reaching Every Farmer

Many farmers are reached best **offline and by voice**, not through ads.

### Ground Network
- **Farmora Mitra (village ambassadors):** one trusted local per village cluster (often a young farmer or SHG member). Onboards farmers, helps with photos and listings, earns a commission on the first 3 months of sales.
- **FPO partnerships:** onboard entire Farmer Producer Organisations at once (bulk honey, millets, pulses).
- **Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) & agriculture universities:** co-host training on crop schedules and AI disease detection.
- **Rythu Bharosa Kendras (AP) / Uzhavan centres (TN) / Kisan Seva Kendras:** posters and demo desks.
- **Demo van ("Farmora Rath"):** visits weekly mandis and village fairs; live app demos, free leaf scans, on-the-spot registration.
- **Panchayat & SHG meetings:** short talks with women's self-help groups (ghee, pickles, spices, honey).

### Digital for Farmers
| Channel | Content |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp channel + groups** (per district, per language) | Daily mandi prices, weather alerts, crop tips, success stories |
| **YouTube** (Telugu, Tamil, Hindi) | "How to sell on Farmora in 5 minutes", crop guides, farmer success stories |
| **ShareChat / Moj** | Short regional videos; very popular in rural India |
| **Community radio & local TV** | Weekly "Farmora Krishi Varta" segment |
| **IVR / missed-call number** | "Give a missed call to register" → callback from an agent in their language |

### Farmer Incentives
- Zero commission for the first 3 months
- Free product photography for the first listing
- "Farmer of the Month" spotlight across all channels
- Referral: farmer brings a farmer → both get a commission waiver

## 8.4 Family Outreach: Reaching Every Household

### Social Media Content Pillars
| Pillar | Example content | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Meet the Farmer** | 60-sec reels: "Ravi from Guntur and his chilli fields" | 30% |
| **Purity & Traceability** | Ghee-making process, honey harvesting, lab test results | 20% |
| **Healthy Recipes** | Millet recipes, traditional dishes by region, tiffin ideas | 20% |
| **Family & Festivals** | Sankranti, Pongal, Diwali, Onam hampers; "cook with your grandma" | 15% |
| **Offers & Community** | Coupons, subscriptions, customer stories, UGC reposts | 15% |

### Platform Playbook
| Platform | Format | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Reels, carousels, stories, Lives with farmers | 1 reel/day, 3 carousels/week |
| YouTube | Long-form farm documentaries + Shorts | 1 long/week, 4 Shorts/week |
| Facebook | Community groups, local events, regional ads | Daily |
| WhatsApp Business | Catalog, order updates, broadcast offers (opt-in only) | 2–3/week max |
| X / LinkedIn | Founder story, impact numbers, investor/partner audience | 3/week |
| Pinterest | Millet and healthy recipe pins | 5/week |

### Growth Tactics
- **Influencers:** regional mom bloggers, food creators, fitness and nutrition creators (micro-influencers with 10k–100k followers rank highest on trust)
- **Apartment & gated communities:** society WhatsApp groups, weekend "farm stall" pop-ups, bulk group orders
- **Referral program:** give ₹100, get ₹100 (built into the app)
- **Subscriptions:** "Monthly Family Pantry Box" (ghee + honey + millets + pulses)
- **Corporate tie-ups:** office wellness hampers, festival gifting
- **Doctors & nutritionists:** millet and diabetes awareness content
- **Performance ads:** Meta + Google + YouTube, geo-targeted to launch cities (Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru, Vijayawada, Coimbatore)

## 8.5 Children: "Little Farmers" Education Program

Reach children **through schools and parents** to build lifelong respect for farmers and healthy food.

| Initiative | Description |
|---|---|
| **School farm visits** | Field trips to Founding Farmers' farms; kids plant a seed and get a "Little Farmer" certificate |
| **"Seed to Plate" school kits** | Free grow-your-own millet/herb kits for classrooms with a 4-week activity booklet (4 languages) |
| **Chinnu the Sprout** | Animated YouTube Kids-safe series: where honey, ghee and dal come from (published as "made for kids", no comments or tracking) |
| **Colouring & story books** | Printable PDFs and physical books distributed at schools and in delivery boxes |
| **Drawing contest** | "My Farmer Hero": entries submitted **by parents/teachers**; winners featured with consent |
| **Family weekend farm days** | Pick-your-own, bullock-cart rides, cooking with farmers |
| **Tiffin recipe series** | Healthy millet snacks for kids, aimed at parents |

**Safeguards:** no child accounts in the app, no ads targeted at under-18s, written parental consent for any child photo or video, school partnerships under MoU.
